About

Engineer first, builder by default

I studied industrial organization engineering and ended up where operations meet software. I joined Cabify in Madrid in November 2025 as a Customer Operations trainee, moved to Operational Excellence in March and became the team's automation analyst in May.

The job is easy to describe: find a process people repeat by hand, build the system that does it, and keep it running. Most of it is n8n with Claude for the reasoning, Python and SQL where it has to be real software, and small models I train myself when an LLM would be overkill. Everything on this page runs, or ran, in production with real users, and each case study says what broke as well as what worked.

I also teach. I was selected as one of the company academy's n8n trainers, and my first session is on 30 September 2026.
